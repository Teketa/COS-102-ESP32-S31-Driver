Overclock

Drivers are rated 30 MHz; with the S3 controller this panel ran stable to ~19.5–20 MHz on both clocks — the ceiling is its HC244 buffers and ribbon, not the driver ICs. The S31 hat adds 33 Ω series resistors, so re-check the limit here.
On the S31 the GCLK comes from a 160 MHz PLL through an integer divider, so it snaps to 40, 26.7, 20, 16, 13.3, 11.4, 10 MHz…; the slider jumps to the rate actually set.

Panel refresh: – · frame rate: –
Higher GCLK = faster PWM refresh (less flicker on camera). Fewer pulses per phase = higher refresh but less grayscale depth. Watch for artifacts; Reset OEM timing restores the known-good values.
